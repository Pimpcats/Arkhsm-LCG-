# Campaign production

The owner wants to experience The Still Hour as a first-time player and do as
little production work as possible. Read docs/ASSISTANT_WORKFLOW.md first.

- Handle writing, rules, scenario assembly, art briefs, generation, framing,
  validation and packaging. Preserve the established campaign and later errata.
- Keep spoilers out of chat, progress summaries, image review labels and PR
  summaries. Do not reveal enemies' roles, twists, solutions, endings or rules
  text. Art review uses neutral labels; flag visually revealing art before
  showing it and keep it out of routine reviews.
- Ask about meaningful creative preferences only when necessary. Do not ask the
  owner to author content, curate filenames or manually maintain manifests.
- Aim for Arkham LCG's painterly cosmic-horror visual language, period detail,
  restrained palette and deliberate composition. Avoid photographic/3D polish,
  repeated texture patterns and incidental generated lettering. Reference actual
  approved artwork when available; do not promise identical results.
- Art approval is not campaign/playtest approval. Report validation honestly.
- Never imply GitHub access gives access to the owner's running desktop app.
