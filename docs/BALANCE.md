# THE STILL HOUR — balance findings (audited)

Generated by `python3 pipeline/simulate.py` (3 players, 20k trials, seed 1729).
This revision is a **full re-audit**: the economy model's dead code was removed,
the "overflow wasted" claim corrected against the actual interlude rules, and the
finale model rebuilt stage-aware (the naive version's headline finding was an
artifact — see the reversal below). Simulation validates the **math**, not the
fun; assumptions are labelled in the script and are **not playtested**.

## Memory (XP) per loop
Shared pool/loop: median **17**, 5–95th **14–20**; carry cap **18**.
- **~30% of loops** earn at/above the carry cap — that Memory **must be spent at
  the interlude or is lost**. (Corrected: the cap binds what you *carry*, not
  what you earn — it is a forced-spend line, not an income ceiling. A spend-all
  party wastes nothing; only hoarding is punished.)
- **~5% of loops** land in the low tail (≤14).
- Campaign total per investigator: 6 loops 34 · 7 loops 40 · 8 loops **45** —
  the official-XP benchmark (40–50) is reached over the 7–8-loop window.

## Aging
Over 7 loops: clean → **Weathered 100%** · typical → **Elder 64% / Ancient 35%**
· reckless → **Aged out 100%**.

Age-out timing (sustained play to 18 Years): reckless **~4.5 loops** · typical
~9 · clean ~18.

## Stress test — hard-reset & the Appointed
Fresh loop, any style: hard-reset **<1%** (Hour IX ends the night first, ~6
rounds); the Appointed reaches **Arrived ~100%** by ~round 5, clock-driven.

Hard-reset % as the scar floor rises:
| scar | cautious | typical | greedy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0% | 0% | 0.6% |
| 2 | 0% | 0% | 6.9% |
| 4 | 0% | 0% | 38% |
| 6 | 0% | 0.6% | **79%** |

## Finale — stage-aware model (supersedes the earlier naive result)
The naive model (95–100% reach) ignored three self-limiters: Hold Back needs the
Appointed **manifest** (pushed to Unseen it can't be farmed), Hour effects
**re-fire on re-crossing** after a rewind (each re-crossed Hour VIII = +2
Dissonance), and Dissonance-18 **loses the finale**. Modelling those:

Reach % (4 deep facts spent):
| declared at Dissonance | p=0.40 | p=0.50 | p=0.60 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | 31% | 55% | 76% |
| 9 | 21% | 41% | 63% |
| 12 (Noticed) | 6% | 14% | 22% |

Deep-facts axis (declared at 9, p=0.50): 3 facts **28%** · 4 **42%** · 5 **56%**.

**Verdict: the finale is genuinely contested and strategically textured** — the
declared-at Dissonance dominates, so *when* you declare matters more than raw
strength, and every deep fact is worth ~14 pts of win rate. R5 ("Next Time")
existing as a soft-fail makes the 40–55% central band reasonable rather than
punishing.

## ⚑ Flags for design (owner's call)

1. **Hard-reset is a campaign pressure, not a per-loop one.** A single loop
   almost never hard-resets (the clock ends it first); the threat compounds via
   the scar (greedy: 0.6% → 79% by scar 6). Matches "the clock wins eventually";
   re-tune per-round Dissonance or loop length if early loops should risk it.

2. **~~The finale looks too easy~~ — RETRACTED.** The stage-aware model shows
   the earlier 95–100% figure was a modelling artifact. As specced, the finale
   sits at ~40–55% for a prepared party declaring at moderate Dissonance, and
   collapses to ~14% if declared in the Noticed band. No change recommended;
   validate at the table.

3. **Sustained reckless play ages out at ~loop 4.5** — at or before the finale
   unlocks (~loop 4). "You cannot end the Still Hour young" is intended, but a
   guaranteed mid-campaign investigator loss for one playstyle is harsh; consider
   whether age-out should soft-land (e.g. the aged-out investigator remains for
   the finale as an NPC-anchor candidate) rather than remove outright.

4. **Typical play drifts Ancient on long campaigns.** At 7 loops, 35% of typical
   players are already Ancient; at 8 loops the typical mean (16 Years) sits in
   Ancient. Aging v0.3 frames Ancient as "reserved for the reckless or for
   overtime" — either that framing or the +2/loop typical rate is off by a loop.

*Model caveats: fixed Hold-Back p and one attempt/investigator/round in the
finale; deck Dissonance held at 0.8/round; income model is Normal-approximate.
Table play validates the rest.*
