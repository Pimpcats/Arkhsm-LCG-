# Current campaign audit — 2026-10-02

Designer reference; contains spoilers. Effective overrides and the current
player guide govern the release. Older design documents are historical.

## Scope and readiness

All 133 effective cards, both playable faces where present, and all eight
scenario boxes were reviewed. There are 14 acts, nine Hours, 23 locations,
five investigators, their 15 required signature/weakness cards and ten
purchasable Recollections. The scenario audit reports eight locked boxes,
zero errors, zero warnings and feasible authored objectives at 1–4 investigators.
A lock is a content check, not human playtest approval.

## Story and continuity

The opening accommodates residents and visitors. Mandatory passages no longer
assume optional district discoveries. Surface discoveries and deep findings
have distinct implications; the Almanac resolution acknowledges information
already available from the Church. District endings cover incomplete, surface,
deep and previously completed work without claiming an unvisited discovery.
Interludes retain choices, Years, banked Memory and persistent Knowledge;
finale outcomes and the aging epilogue are conditional on actual recorded
state. No ending or branch was removed to force a ten-scenario line.

## Hourglass and acts

The nine Hour fronts and story backs agree with their transitions. Hour I
requires 3 doom; Hours II–VIII require 2 each: **17 advancing doom**. Hour IX
is terminal and has neither an advance threshold nor a further transition.
Skips, crossings and encounters also affect tempo, so 17 doom is not a promise
of 17 rounds. Travel places doom on the current Hour on the first move along
each district connection each round; the Road–Lighthouse connection is free.

Clue costs below are payments, not necessarily printed act-advance thresholds.
Take-and-deliver acts pay to obtain a story asset and advance at delivery.
The Church surface delivery additionally waits for a completed Hour advance.
The fare is a mixed payment and must be fully affordable before any effect.

## Balance and release evidence

The ten targets map to **played order**: Prologue, eight nights, finale. This
campaign remains nonlinear, with repeatable nights and persistent completed
districts. Representative scenario presets are conditional states and use
budgeted decks; they do not prove those purchases were paid from earlier
trials. Carried runs separately preserve actual earnings, spending, Years,
Knowledge and weaknesses and never inject an unearned finale unlock.

The current difficulty evidence and confidence intervals are in
[FINISHING_BALANCE.md](FINISHING_BALANCE.md); release checks and dispositions
are in [FINISHING_CORRECTIONS.md](FINISHING_CORRECTIONS.md). Review
records and raw simulation evidence are designer-only. Human table testing
still establishes clarity, fun and the real success curve.

## Lessons retained from earlier campaign comparisons

The earlier official-campaign comparison remains a dated structural reference,
not a human win-rate benchmark. Its useful checks are retained here: a story
branch may assume only its actual prerequisites; failure must have an authored
continuation; clue payments must fit the open map; optional Victory must cost
time; enemies and test-based encounters must both contribute pressure; and XP
must be earned before purchases. The corrections above apply those checks to
the current campaign. Repeatable loops, aging and custom Memory remain deliberate
campaign mechanics, so matching another campaign's median does not establish
their balance.
