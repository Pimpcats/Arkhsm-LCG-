## 1. HOW THIS CAMPAIGN WORKS

The Still Hour is not played as a line of separate scenarios. It is played as **one night, lived over and over.**

- A **loop** is one continuous play-through of the night across the shared Ambergrove map. The **Hourglass** (the Occultation clock, Hours I–IX) is the master timer for the whole loop.
- You travel between **districts** during the loop. Each district is a set-piece with things to learn and do. You will only reach **2–3 districts** before time runs out.
- When the Hourglass reaches **Hour IX — The Appointed Hour**, or when every investigator is defeated, or when Dissonance hits **18**, the loop **resets.** The board wipes and everyone is healed — but **Memory, the Knowledge Track, Dissonance's scar, and Years all carry forward.**
- Between loops you play an **interlude** (§4): age the investigators, bank Memory, buy Recollections.
- You are trying to learn enough of the night (the **Knowledge Track**) to reach and survive **The Last Hour** — knowing that every loop costs you years.

**The board persists within a loop.** You are living one relentless night; damage, resources, and cards in play do **not** reset when you travel between districts. The reset is your only breather — and your only checkpoint. *(This refines the between-nodes carry-over line in `cards v0.2 §A`: within a loop, the board persists; only a reset wipes it.)*

**Acts are progression gates, not separate scenarios:**
- **Act I — Learning the Rules** (roughly loops 1–3): only **surface** objectives are available; the Latecomer sleeps.
- **Act II — The Shape of the Hour** (once **3+ surface facts** are known): **deep** objectives open; the Latecomer can arrive; the Whisper cards enter the encounter deck.
- **The Last Hour** (once **"The Way the Night Breaks"** is known): the finale may be attempted.

## 2. CAMPAIGN SPECIAL RULES (in play order)

**Setup each loop.**
1. Set the Hourglass to **Hour I**. Arrange the Occultation deck (Hours I–IX) in order, applying every Knowledge edit currently flagged on the log (`encounter v0.4 §1`).
2. Set **Dissonance** to the **scar floor** = number of completed loops so far, maximum **6**.
3. Build the chaos bag for the current Dissonance **band** (Calm 0–5, Glitch 6–11, Noticed 12–17; add `[static]` tokens per band — `encounter v0.4 §0`).
4. Build the encounter deck: the shared spine (`encounter v0.4 §2,§3,§6`) + the node sets of any districts you intend to visit + (Act II onward) the Whispers. Set the Latecomer aside.
5. Place all investigators at **The Square**. Return any Memory on cards to its owner (Memory carries as **banked**, not on cards, between loops).

**Memory.** Tokens earned during the loop sit on cards; at the interlude they bank. **Banked Memory is capped at 18** at the start of each loop (6 × investigators). Spent only on Recollections. Two distinct pools — *on-card* (in-scenario fuel) and *banked* (campaign currency) — are always named explicitly.

**Dissonance.** Shared track, 0–18. Rises when you act on foreknowledge (playing Recollections, foreknowledge abilities, and certain encounter cards). At **12** the Latecomer arrives; at **18** the loop resets. It only falls via specific cards.

**The Hourglass & the Occultation.** The clock advances ~1 Hour of its own accord each round, plus any **Skip** effects (including **travel between districts, which costs 1 Hour**). Each Hour reached resolves its Occultation effect in order.

**Knowledge Track.** Facts are unlocked by completing district objectives (never bought). Flagged facts permanently edit the night — Hours, locations, the encounter deck, and the finale (§7).

**Aging.** At each interlude every investigator gains **Years** (`aging v0.3 §1`): +1 base, +1 if defeated that loop, +1 if the loop ended at Dissonance 12+, +1 if they leaned on the loop (raised Dissonance 3+ times or spent 4+ Memory). Brackets: **Prime 0–4, Weathered 5–9, Elder 10–14, Ancient 15+**, each with its drift. At **18 Years** an investigator ages out.

**Echoes & the Latecomer.** Echoes are **Sleepwalking** in the Calm band and wake in Glitch+. The Latecomer cannot be defeated, only **Held Back** to buy time (`encounter v0.4 §5`).

## 3. THE AMBERGROVE MAP

A small inland town under a starless, snagged sky. **The Square** is the hub; travel along a connection costs **1 Hour (a Skip)**.

```
                 THE LIGHTHOUSE
                       |
                 THE SUNKEN ROAD
                       |
   THE FAIRGROUND — THE SQUARE — THE DROWNED CHURCH
                       |
                 THE ALMANAC HOUSE
```

- **The Square** connects to: the Fairground, the Drowned Church, the Almanac House, the Sunken Road.
- **The Sunken Road** connects the Square to the **Lighthouse**.
- Each district is a cluster of 2–4 locations, revealed when you first enter it that loop.

## 4. THE INTERLUDE PROCEDURE (run after every reset)

1. **Age.** Each investigator adds their **Years** for the loop just ended. Apply any bracket change now (choose the locked physical/mental skills the first time you enter Weathered). Any investigator reaching 18 Years ages out.
2. **Bank Memory.** Move all on-card Memory to banked Memory. Reduce banked Memory to **18** if higher (you can't hold more of the night than that).
3. **Spend.** Spend banked Memory as experience, from the shared pool: buy Recollections (at their `memoryCost`) **and/or** level up eligible cards (at 1 Memory per card level, respecting each investigator's deckbuilding access). Upgraded variants replace their base.
4. **Record.** Update Dissonance's scar (= loops completed, max 6), the Knowledge Track, the dead, and any choice flags.
5. **Read** the interlude beat for the current Act (below), then begin the next loop at §2 setup.

**Interlude beats (narrative):**
- *After Loop 1:* You wake at First Dark with the taste of salt and the certainty you have done this before. The others feel it too. No one in Ambergrove will believe you.
- *Entering Act II (3+ surface facts):* The town has begun to notice that you notice. Somewhere past the edge of the Square, something that has been waiting politely decides to stop waiting.
- *Before the Finale:* You know the shape of the night now. You know what it costs to end it. The only question left is who pays.

## 5. PROLOGUE — THE FIRST HOUR

> *The almanac promised the occultation at midnight. It is eleven, and the sky is already wrong — the stars sliding behind something that has no edge. You came to Ambergrove for your own reasons. You will not remember why, soon. You will remember everything else.*

**Teaching scenario. Linear. Ends in a scripted first reset.**

**Setup.** Place three locations in a row: **The Square**, **The Long Pier**, **The Almanac Steps**. Investigators begin at the Square. Hourglass at Hour I. Dissonance 0. Encounter deck: shared spine only (no Whispers, no Latecomer). Chaos bag: Calm band.

**Locations.**
- **The Square** — shroud 1, 2 clues. *"Everyone is out for the occultation. None of them will look at you twice — not this time, not ever."*
- **The Long Pier** — shroud 2, 2 clues.
- **The Almanac Steps** — shroud 2, 3 clues. Objective location.

**Objective.** Gather 5 clues total, then an investigator at the Almanac Steps may **resolve the Occultation** (no test). When they do, read the ending. If the Hourglass reaches Hour IX first, read the ending anyway.

**Scripted ending — "The First Reset."**
> *The occultation completes. Something reaches through — and does not finish reaching. The night folds. You are standing in the Square again at eleven o'clock, salt on your tongue, and you are the only ones who feel the seam.*

- Each investigator gains **2 banked Memory** (their first).
- Record the fact **"You Are Unstuck"** on the log (Act I begins).
- No Years are gained from the Prologue. Proceed to the first interlude, then Loop 1.

> **Design note.** The Prologue can't be "lost" — its job is to teach Memory carry-over and the reset. Whether you gather the clues or run out of time, you loop. Clever play just banks the clues as understanding (a small bonus Memory if you finished the objective: +1).

## 6. THE SIX DISTRICTS

Each district has a **surface objective** (Act I) and a **deep objective** (Act II, available only after the district's surface fact is known and Act II has begun). Each grants a **Knowledge fact.** Each adds a small **node encounter set** to the deck when you intend to visit it.

---

### 6.1 THE LIGHTHOUSE
> *Elias's lighthouse. The lamp is dark. It has never, in any loop, been lit — and Elias cannot remember why he didn't light it.*

**Locations.** The Lantern Room (shroud 3, 2 clues) · The Winding Stair (shroud 2, 1 clue) · The Keeper's Quarters (shroud 2, 2 clues).
**Node set (2):** *The Dark That Waits* (treachery, horror at the Lantern Room) ×1; *Something on the Stair* (Echo enemy, low stats, Sleepwalking) ×1.

**Surface objective — "light the lamp."** Discover 4 clues across the district, then test `[com]` or `[wil]` (3) at the Lantern Room to light the lamp.
→ Unlock **"The Lamp Was Never Lit."** *Edit:* while the lamp is lit this loop, Echoes at connected districts stay Sleepwalking one band longer (light holds the town still). Permanent: the Lighthouse's Lantern Room flips to its calmer back for all future loops.

**Deep objective — "the ninth death."** (Act II) With the lamp lit, search the Keeper's Quarters (2 clues) for the keeper's logbook. Elias reads his own handwriting recording eight prior deaths.
→ Unlock **"The Keeper's Ninth Death"** (Elias thread). *Edit:* Elias's elder-sign heal also removes 1 horror. *Finale:* enables the **Break Through** resolution to save all investigators rather than one.

---

### 6.2 THE DROWNED CHURCH
> *The bell tolled thirteen times at the first occultation. It should toll twelve. The thirteenth is the sound the night is stuck on.*

**Locations.** The Nave (shroud 2, 3 clues) · The Belfry (shroud 3, 2 clues) · The Flooded Crypt (shroud 4, 3 clues — sealed until a fact opens it) · The Vestry (shroud 2, 2 clues).
**Node set (3):** *The Congregation Kneels* (Drowned Choir Echo, from `encounter v0.4 §3`) ×1; *Thirteen* (treachery: Skip +1 and raise Dissonance 1 if at the Church) ×1; *Rising Water* (treachery: a location gains shroud +1 for the round) ×1.

**Surface objective — "why thirteen?"** Discover 6 clues, then read the register in the Vestry.
→ Unlock **"The Thirteenth Toll."** *Edit (already printed on Hour III):* the Church no longer adds its extra +1 Dissonance at the Toll.

**Deep objective — "the hour was wrong."** (Act II) With the Toll known, the **Flooded Crypt** opens. Recover the drowned almanac-page (3 clues in the Crypt).
→ Unlock **"The Hour Was Wrong."** *Edit:* **Hour IV — The Road Gives Way — is removed from the Occultation deck** for all future loops. This is one of the single most valuable facts (it stops the mid-loop road collapse forever).

---

### 6.3 THE SUNKEN ROAD
> *The road between the town and the shore. The same figures walk it every loop, in the same direction, never arriving. Some of them turn to look at you now.*

**Locations.** The Milestones (shroud 1, 2 clues) · The Low Bridge (shroud 3, 2 clues) · The Turning (shroud 2, 3 clues).
**Node set (2):** *Those Who Walk* (Waiting Congregation Echo) ×1; *The Bridge Remembers* (treachery: if you cross the Low Bridge this round, Skip +1) ×1.

**Surface objective — "walk it backward."** Move through all three locations in reverse of the Echoes' direction (Turning → Bridge → Milestones), discovering at least 1 clue at each.
→ Unlock **"The Road Remembers."** *Edit:* travel to/from the Lighthouse via the Sunken Road costs 0 Hours once per loop (you know the shortcut).

**Deep objective — "who walks beside you."** (Act II) At the Turning, evade or Hold an awakened Echo and read the face beneath the hood.
→ Unlock **"Who Walks Beside You"** (the Echoes are the town's earlier loopers, worn to nothing). *Edit:* Echoes get −1 Fight against investigators who have this fact (you know how to speak to them). *Finale:* softens the **Take Its Place** ending.

---

### 6.4 THE SQUARE (Town Hall)
> *The hub of the night. The sheriff stands on the Town Hall steps giving the same speech to the same crowd. He has been dead since ten o'clock. No one has noticed but you.*

**Locations.** The Town Hall Steps (shroud 2, 2 clues) · The Records Office (shroud 3, 3 clues) · The Well (shroud 2, 2 clues). *(The Square is always the loop's start location.)*
**Node set (2):** *The Same Speech* (treachery: Peril, horror scaling with loops completed) ×1; *The Crowd Turns* (treachery: each Echo in play readies) ×1.

**Surface objective — "the sheriff is already dead."** Discover 5 clues, then examine the body at the Well.
→ Unlock **"The Sheriff Is Already Dead."** *Edit:* the Town Hall Steps flip to a back that lets you draw the top Occultation card once per loop (the records let you read the night ahead) — synergizes with Seraphine/Ayako.

**Deep objective — "the vote that never ends."** (Act II) In the Records Office, find the ledger showing the town *voted* to open the door.
→ Unlock **"The Vote That Never Ends"** + advances **Seraphine's thread** ("Who Opened the Door"). *Edit:* reveals that ending the loop cleanly requires a *cost the town agreed to pay* — foreshadows the finale's price. *Finale:* required input for **Close the Door.**

---

### 6.5 THE FAIRGROUND
> *The occultation fair, mid-celebration, forever. The wheel turns. The ticket-taker smiles at you like he's been expecting you for a very long time.*

**Locations.** The Wheel (shroud 3, 2 clues) · The Hall of Mirrors (shroud 2, 3 clues) · The Ticket Booth (shroud 2, 2 clues).
**Node set (2):** *The Wheel's Turn* (treachery: Skip +1, but each investigator gains 1 resource) ×1; *Your Reflection Lies* (treachery: Hall of Mirrors, horror + a `[static]` token) ×1.

**Surface objective — "the wheel still turns."** Ride the Wheel: an investigator at the Wheel tests `[agi]` (3); on success, discover 2 clues from a height (you see the whole town at once).
→ Unlock **"The Wheel Still Turns."** *Edit:* once per loop, from the Wheel, you may look at the top 2 encounter cards and reorder them.

**Deep objective — "the ticket-taker's bargain."** (Act II) Speak with the ticket-taker at the Booth; he offers a way to end the night that is not free.
→ Unlock **"The Ticket-Taker's Bargain"** (the Faustian thread). *Finale:* unlocks the **Let It In, On Your Terms** resolution — but flags the log that the bargain was heard (affects the epilogue tone).

---

### 6.6 THE ALMANAC HOUSE
> *Where the wrong almanac was printed. Ayako has read every book in it a hundred times. In one loop she found a page that was not there in the others.*

**Locations.** The Reading Room (shroud 2, 2 clues) · The Press (shroud 3, 3 clues) · The Sealed Study (shroud 4, 3 clues — opens with a fact).
**Node set (3):** *The Page That Wasn't* (treachery: Ayako-relevant; horror scaling with banked Memory) ×1; *Ink Runs Backward* (treachery: cancel a clue discovery this round) ×1; *The Study Door* (treachery: while the Study is sealed, adds a `[static]`) ×1.

**Surface objective — "what the almanac hid."** Discover 6 clues, then set the Press to reprint the true occultation time.
→ Unlock **"What the Almanac Hid."** *Edit (printed on Hour VI):* at the Wrong Sky, investigators *remove* a `[static]` instead of adding one.

**Deep objective — "the latecomer's name."** (Act II, requires **"What the Almanac Hid"** + **"The Vote That Never Ends"**) The **Sealed Study** opens. Inside is the name the town spoke to call the thing through.
→ Unlock **"The Latecomer's Name"** + completes **"Who Opened the Door"** (Seraphine's thread). *Edit (printed on Hour VII):* the Latecomer arrives exhausted. *Finale:* required input for every resolution except **The Loop Wins**; naming it is what makes the crossing contestable.

**Assembling the finale.** When you know **"The Latecomer's Name,"** **"The Vote That Never Ends,"** and any **one** other deep fact, unlock **"The Way the Night Breaks."** The finale may now be attempted.

## 8. ACT TRANSITIONS

- **Act I → Act II** triggers at the interlude after you hold **3+ surface facts** (or after completing Loop 3, whichever comes first). From the next loop: deep objectives open; add the **Whisper** cards; the Latecomer may arrive at the Noticed band.
- **The Last Hour** becomes attemptable once **"The Way the Night Breaks"** is known. You may keep looping to prepare (banking Memory, buying Recollections, and — knowingly — aging) or attempt it.

## 9. FINALE — THE LAST HOUR

> *You have lived this night more times than you can hold. You know the toll, the road, the wrong sky, the name. You know what the town agreed to. All that's left is to be there when it arrives — and to decide what you're willing to spend.*

**Attempting the finale.** During any loop after **"The Way the Night Breaks"** is known, an investigator who reaches the **Almanac House Sealed Study** may declare **The Last Hour** (or it triggers automatically if you let the Hourglass reach Hour IX while the fact is known — `encounter v0.4 §1`).

**Setup.** The Latecomer enters play immediately if not already present, at the location farthest from the investigators. Dissonance does not reset. Play proceeds under a single objective: **contest the crossing** — accumulate **"contest" progress** equal to **4 × investigators (12 at three players)** by Holding Back the Latecomer, spending Knowledge, and reaching the Study, before the Hourglass reaches Hour IX. Each **Hold Back** success and each deep fact spent adds 1 contest progress.

**Resolution generator.** When the finale ends (contest reached, or Hour IX, or all defeated), read the resolution matched by the campaign log, checked **top to bottom** (use the first that qualifies):

| # | Condition | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Contest reached **and** an **Ancient** investigator is present | **Take Its Place.** One Ancient becomes the night's new anchor — kept behind, decades older, so the loop ends for everyone else. The town wakes for the first time in a hundred years. *(Softened if "Who Walks Beside You" is known.)* |
| R2 | Contest reached **and** you know **The Vote That Never Ends** + **The Latecomer's Name** | **Close the Door.** You pay the price the town agreed to and never paid: everyone present ages **+3 Years at once**, and the door closes. The cleanest true ending — bought with years. |
| R3 | Contest reached **and** **The Keeper's Ninth Death** is known | **Break Through.** You force the night open and all survivors escape — but the loop is only *paused*. Bittersweet epilogue: was it really ended? |
| R4 | Contest reached (no special facts) | **Seal by Force.** The door is sealed but not closed; one investigator (chosen) ages out doing it. |
| R5 | Not reached, but **banked Memory ≥ 12** | **Next Time.** The loop wins tonight — but you wake at First Dark closer than ever. Begin a new loop; the finale may be re-attempted. |
| R6 | Not reached, banked Memory < 12 | **The Loop Wins.** The night takes you. The campaign ends. The town keeps repeating — and somewhere, five faces join the ones on the Sunken Road. |

> **Design note.** Every "contest reached" branch is a *win with a different cost*, and the cost is always age or an investigator kept behind — the finale you asked for. R5 makes even failure a soft continue as long as you've been remembering. R6 is the only true loss, reserved for a party that never banked the night.

## 10. DIFFICULTY & SCALING

- **3 investigators (baseline):** all constants as printed (Dissonance 12/18, soft cap 18, contest 12, scar cap 6).
- **1–2 or 4 investigators:** Dissonance thresholds **4× / 6× investigators**; Memory soft cap **6× investigators**; contest **4× investigators**; scar cap = ⅓ reset threshold. Standalone Standard/Hard chaos-bag presets ship with the token spec.
- **Campaign length:** tuned for ~6–8 loops; clean play arrives at the finale ~Weathered, typical ~Elder. Shortening the campaign = raise district clue counts (fewer loops to unlock facts); lengthening = the reverse.

*All values provisional until table play; simulation validates pacing and economy, not drama.*
