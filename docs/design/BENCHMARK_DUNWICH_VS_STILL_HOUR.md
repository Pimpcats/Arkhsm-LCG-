# Benchmark: The Dunwich Legacy's first three scenarios vs The Still Hour (same engine, same AI)

Designer-facing. The Still Hour appears under neutral labels only (Prologue, Night 1–8, Finale; each Night pools every district's scenario for that night, 10 runs per district). Generated by `tools/play_engine/official/dwl_docs.py`, commit c82eea7, 2026-11-10. Simulation, not a playtest.

## Conditions

- Same table (SCED in the TTS emulator), same rules layer and the same AI action scoring for both; Standard difficulty; party sizes 2, 3 and 4.
- Same level-0 cards: the Dunwich investigators play The Still Hour's starter cards of their class (Zoey ← the Guardian list, Rex ← the Seeker list, Jenny ← the Rogue list, Jim ← the Mystic list). The party differs: The Still Hour runs seat its own investigators (2p Guardian + Seeker, 3p + Rogue, 4p + Mystic, `run.py PARTIES`), Dunwich seats Zoey, Rex, Jenny, Jim with their own stats, abilities, signature cards and weaknesses. The Still Hour's nights after the Prologue use its representative XP decks (`decks.py TIERS`, small upgrades from Night 1); Dunwich 1a/1b are level 0 (as at the start of a campaign).
- Dunwich's rows are 1a Extracurricular Activity, 1b The House Always Wins and 2 The Miskatonic Museum, each played on its own. The Museum is the campaign's third scenario: its row here is the standalone one (level-0 decks, no trauma, the plain Standard bag), like for like with 1a/1b; played after 1a and 1b with what they leave behind it goes worse (docs/design/DUNWICH_SIM_REPORT.md, campaign order).
- The Still Hour was played through `official/benchmark.py`, which runs run.py's engine unchanged and only counts skill tests by kind (for the encounter-test metric). Its games here were played on 2026-11-06 and were not replayed when the Museum was added (2026-11-10): changes made to The Still Hour since then are not in its rows.
- Metric definitions: *win* = Dunwich: a resolution by the scenario's objectives (R1–R3; the Museum R1/R2); The Still Hour: the night's goal acts all advanced (`report.py` completion), the Finale: the forms defeated (ended 'boss'; a stranded form is a partial ending, counted as a loss here). *Losses*: time/agenda = the agenda deck ran out (Dunwich R4; in the Museum the last agenda advanced or was about to and the survivors left by the door) or Hour IX / the reset came first (Still Hour); defeated = every investigator defeated. *Rounds* = rounds played (a Still Hour loop always runs to its end, so *round won* is also shown). *Clues required* = the clue cost of the goal acts (Dunwich: the act clues plus the location objective the AI goes for; the Museum: the Entrance's, 1 per investigator for each Exhibit Hall down to the Restricted Hall, and the Restricted Hall's own, averaged over the games). *Rounds per agenda* = rounds per agenda advanced (Dunwich) / per Hour turned (Still Hour). *Encounter tests* = skill tests on treachery cards per game, failures in brackets. *XP* = Dunwich XP earned per investigator (Victory X plus the resolution's bonus); Still Hour: Memory banked by the party per loop (its experience currency; not the same scale).

## Headline

- **2p**: the AI wins Dunwich's first scenarios 13% (1a, 1b and the Museum pooled; 1a 23%, 1b 12%, the Museum 5%) and The Still Hour's Prologue and nights 79% (pooled); the Finale 47%. Damage + horror per investigator per round: Dunwich 1.10, Still Hour nights 0.81; defeats per game: Dunwich 1.71, Still Hour nights 0.55.
- **3p**: the AI wins Dunwich's first scenarios 24% (1a, 1b and the Museum pooled; 1a 33%, 1b 27%, the Museum 13%) and The Still Hour's Prologue and nights 83% (pooled); the Finale 0%. Damage + horror per investigator per round: Dunwich 0.93, Still Hour nights 0.61; defeats per game: Dunwich 2.32, Still Hour nights 0.53.
- **4p**: the AI wins Dunwich's first scenarios 20% (1a, 1b and the Museum pooled; 1a 27%, 1b 18%, the Museum 15%) and The Still Hour's Prologue and nights 77% (pooled); the Finale 3%. Damage + horror per investigator per round: Dunwich 0.93, Still Hour nights 0.79; defeats per game: Dunwich 3.03, Still Hour nights 0.56.
- As this AI plays them, The Still Hour's Prologue and nights are markedly easier than Dunwich's first three scenarios and gentler on the investigators (fewer defeats, less damage and horror per round). The Finale is harder than the baseline at 3p, 4p. Most of Dunwich's losses are total defeats; most of The Still Hour's are running out of time.
- **The Miskatonic Museum** (the third Dunwich point: a long clue hunt with one recurring Elite enemy): 2p win 5% (agenda 15%, all defeated 80%), 13.3 rounds, 1.72 defeats / game, damage + horror 0.88 per investigator per round, 0.40 clues per investigator per round against 16.1 required (Still Hour nights: win 79%, 12.2 rounds, 0.55 defeats, 0.81 damage + horror, 0.55 clues against 9.1); 3p win 13% (agenda 30%, all defeated 57%), 14.8 rounds, 2.18 defeats / game, damage + horror 0.77 per investigator per round, 0.42 clues per investigator per round against 22.9 required (Still Hour nights: win 83%, 10.6 rounds, 0.53 defeats, 0.61 damage + horror, 0.65 clues against 13.7); 4p win 15% (agenda 50%, all defeated 35%), 15.6 rounds, 2.42 defeats / game, damage + horror 0.72 per investigator per round, 0.38 clues per investigator per round against 29.4 required (Still Hour nights: win 77%, 9.5 rounds, 0.56 defeats, 0.79 damage + horror, 0.71 clues against 18.2). It runs longer than 1a/1b and asks for more clues than any Still Hour night, and it is the one Dunwich scenario here where the agenda takes a real share of the losses.

## Reading The Still Hour's targets through Dunwich (the AI-to-official ratio)

The Still Hour's balance targets were set as AI completion rates (docs/design/PLAYTEST_SIM.md: about 80% on Night 1 falling to 50–60% late). The same AI wins Dunwich's first three scenarios at the rate above. No sourced human win rate for these scenarios is in the downloaded data, so the human side can only be read conditionally: if a typical group wins Dunwich's first three scenarios at H, the AI's gap to people on an official scenario is logit(H) − logit(AI). Applying the same gap to a Still Hour scenario gives the rate a typical group would be expected to reach there, assuming the AI's weaknesses cost it as much in The Still Hour as in Dunwich (they may cost less: The Still Hour's AI and objectives were tuned together).
