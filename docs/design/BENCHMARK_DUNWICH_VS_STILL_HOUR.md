# Benchmark: The Dunwich Legacy's opening pair vs The Still Hour (same engine, same AI)

Designer-facing. The Still Hour appears under neutral labels only (Prologue, Night 1–8, Finale; each Night pools every district's scenario for that night, 10 runs per district). Generated by `tools/play_engine/official/dwl_docs.py`, commit 729d30d, 2026-11-06. Simulation, not a playtest.

## Conditions

- Same table (SCED in the TTS emulator), same rules layer and the same AI action scoring for both; Standard difficulty; party sizes 2, 3 and 4.
- Same level-0 cards: the Dunwich investigators play The Still Hour's starter cards of their class (Zoey ← the Guardian list, Rex ← the Seeker list, Jenny ← the Rogue list, Jim ← the Mystic list). The party differs: The Still Hour runs seat its own investigators (2p Guardian + Seeker, 3p + Rogue, 4p + Mystic, `run.py PARTIES`), Dunwich seats Zoey, Rex, Jenny, Jim with their own stats, abilities, signature cards and weaknesses. The Still Hour's nights after the Prologue use its representative XP decks (`decks.py TIERS`, small upgrades from Night 1); Dunwich 1a/1b are level 0 (as at the start of a campaign).
- The Still Hour was played through `official/benchmark.py`, which runs run.py's engine unchanged and only counts skill tests by kind (for the encounter-test metric).
- Metric definitions: *win* = Dunwich: a resolution by the scenario's objectives (R1–R3); The Still Hour: the night's goal acts all advanced (`report.py` completion), the Finale: the forms defeated (ended 'boss'; a stranded form is a partial ending, counted as a loss here). *Losses*: time/agenda = the agenda deck ran out (Dunwich R4) or Hour IX / the reset came first (Still Hour); defeated = every investigator defeated. *Rounds* = rounds played (a Still Hour loop always runs to its end, so *round won* is also shown). *Clues required* = the clue cost of the goal acts (Dunwich: the act clues plus the location objective the AI goes for). *Rounds per agenda* = rounds per agenda advanced (Dunwich) / per Hour turned (Still Hour). *Encounter tests* = skill tests on treachery cards per game, failures in brackets. *XP* = Dunwich XP earned per investigator (Victory X plus the resolution's bonus); Still Hour: Memory banked by the party per loop (its experience currency; not the same scale).

## Headline

- **2p**: the AI wins Dunwich's opener 18% (1a and 1b pooled) and The Still Hour's Prologue and nights 79% (pooled); the Finale 47%. Damage + horror per investigator per round: Dunwich 1.21, Still Hour nights 0.81; defeats per game: Dunwich 1.70, Still Hour nights 0.55.
- **3p**: the AI wins Dunwich's opener 30% (1a and 1b pooled) and The Still Hour's Prologue and nights 83% (pooled); the Finale 0%. Damage + horror per investigator per round: Dunwich 1.02, Still Hour nights 0.61; defeats per game: Dunwich 2.38, Still Hour nights 0.53.
- **4p**: the AI wins Dunwich's opener 22% (1a and 1b pooled) and The Still Hour's Prologue and nights 77% (pooled); the Finale 3%. Damage + horror per investigator per round: Dunwich 1.04, Still Hour nights 0.79; defeats per game: Dunwich 3.33, Still Hour nights 0.56.
- As this AI plays them, The Still Hour's Prologue and nights are markedly easier than Dunwich's opening pair and gentler on the investigators (fewer defeats, less damage and horror per round). The Finale is harder than the baseline at 3p, 4p. Most of Dunwich's losses are total defeats; most of The Still Hour's are running out of time.

## Reading The Still Hour's targets through Dunwich (the AI-to-official ratio)

The Still Hour's balance targets were set as AI completion rates (docs/design/PLAYTEST_SIM.md: about 80% on Night 1 falling to 50–60% late). The same AI wins Dunwich's opener at the rate above. No sourced human win rate for these scenarios is in the downloaded data, so the human side can only be read conditionally: if a typical group wins Dunwich's opener at H, the AI's gap to people on an official scenario is logit(H) − logit(AI). Applying the same gap to a Still Hour scenario gives the rate a typical group would be expected to reach there, assuming the AI's weaknesses cost it as much in The Still Hour as in Dunwich (they may cost less: The Still Hour's AI and objectives were tuned together).
