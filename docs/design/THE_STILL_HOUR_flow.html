The Still Hour · campaign flow

One night, on repeathow a single evening powers the whole campaign

There is no line of scenarios. You play one night, the loop resets, and you begin again — carrying what you remembered. Everything below hangs off that one turning wheel.

the engine

The Recurrence

  1. 1 · Wake

    First Dark. The night restarts — and only your five remember it.

  2. 2 · Play the night

    Travel the districts. Crack objectives for Knowledge; earn Memory as you go.

  3. 3 · The hour closes

    The clock runs out, you fall, or Dissonance hits 18. The loop resets.

  4. 4 · Interlude

    Age. Bank your Memory. Spend it on Recollections. Then wake again.

the tension

Two forces on the wheel

Knowledge pulls forward

Facts only come from cracking districts, and you can't crack them all in one night. You must loop — and only Knowledge makes the finale reachable.

vs
The price pulls back

Using foreknowledge raises Dissonance, which wakes the Latecomer. And every loop ages you. Getting stronger is spending time you can't get back.

Where they meet is the finale — and the whole game is choosing when you've learned enough to be worth the years it cost.

the xp engine

Memory → Recollections

→
Bank

At the interlude it banks, capped at 18. You can't hoard a whole night.

→
Spend

Buy Recollections — your XP cards — into any deck.

→
Stronger

Wake next loop with more of the night at your command.

The catch

Recollections are foreknowledge — playing them raises Dissonance. Your power source and your danger source are the same resource. Spend boldly and the loop notices you.

the payoff

The Last Hour

The cost is age.

Knowledge decides whether you can end the night at all. Your Years decide how it ends — and who pays. A young party forces an escape; an old one closes the door for good; an Ancient can stay behind as the night's new anchor so the others walk free. You always win differently, and you always win older.
