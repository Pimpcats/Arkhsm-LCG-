# The Dunwich Legacy on the play engine (1a, 1b)

Designer tooling report (no Still Hour content). Generated by `tools/play_engine/official/dwl_docs.py` from games played by `tools/play_engine/official/dwl.py` (`python3 tools/play_engine/run.py --official dwl ...`), commit 729d30d, 2026-11-06. Simulation, not a playtest: one AI party, read the numbers as how *this AI* fares, not how a table of people would.

## What was played

- **The table.** The same headless SCED table The Still Hour's engine uses (tests/sced_real: SCED's own scripts in the TTS emulator). SCED's own Dunwich Legacy campaign box (downloaded, `.cache/official/dwl_sced.json`, never committed) is spawned and its *Place* button lays out the scenario boxes; each game respawns the scenario box from that data and clicks its *Place* (SCED's MemoryBag script), so the locations, agenda/act decks, encounter deck and set-aside cards are SCED's real objects. Clues appear when the engine flips a location (SCED spawns them, per investigator), encounter cards are drawn with the playmat's button, chaos tokens come from SCED's bag filled by the box's own Standard difficulty tile. SCED's token-spawn tracker is reset between games, as its Clean Up helper does.
- **The cards.** Every card the two boxes hold (68 ids, including the core-set encounter sets the scenarios use) has an entry in `lua/official/dwl_effects.lua`, read from ArkhamDB's card data (stats and text) and the campaign guide data (arkham-cards-data: resolutions, log entries, XP). Missing: none.
- **The rules.** The Rules Reference round (mythos doom and encounter draws from round 2, investigation, enemy, upkeep), agenda doom (doom on enemies counts), act clue thresholds, hunters (nearest first, Prey breaks ties), aloof, massive, retaliate, surge, peril, attacks of opportunity, resignation, defeat trauma (damage: physical, horror: mental). No Still Hour systems (Hours, Dissonance, Memory): `lua/official/dwl_rules.lua` replaces them for this mode only; The Still Hour's engine files are not modified.
- **The investigators.** Dunwich's own: Zoey Samaras, Rex Murphy, Jenny Barnes, Jim Culver (abilities, elder signs, signature cards and weaknesses encoded in `lua/official/dwl_players.lua`; "Ashcan" Pete was left out: Duke and his ready ability are not encoded). Parties: 2p Zoey + Rex, 3p + Jenny, 4p + Jim. Each plays the 30 level-0 cards of The Still Hour's starter deck of the same class (Guardian, Seeker, Rogue, Mystic: legal for them, 5 off-class cards each; Jim plays 2 Holy Rosary instead of the unique Jim Culver ally) plus one random basic weakness, so the card pool and the AI's card play are the ones the Still Hour runs use.
- **The AI.** The Still Hour's action scoring (`lua/ai.lua`) with the scenario's objectives and actions added around it (`lua/official/dwl_ai.lua`): where the act's clues are, finding and parleying "Jazz", the Faculty Offices as the goal (R1) when Extracurricular Activity is played first, the Dormitories (R2) when it is second, the Clover Club's clue actions in act 1, exploring the Back Hall Doorways, parleying Dr. Morgan / Peter Clover, then resigning; resigning before the last agenda runs out. It never "cheats" with Beginner's Luck unless a run asks for it (`--cheat`).

## Headline

- **1a Extracurricular Activity**: the AI wins 2p 23%, 3p 33%, 4p 27% (standalone). Almost every win is R1 at the Faculty Offices; when it loses, it is mostly every investigator defeated, not the agenda. Main causes of defeat: Wizard of Yog-Sothoth (36% of defeats), Yithian Observer (17% of defeats), Beyond the Veil (10% of defeats).
- **1b The House Always Wins**: the AI wins 2p 12%, 3p 27%, 4p 18%. It loses by being defeated before it resigns, mostly in act 2 (finding the VIP Area): Clover Club Pit Boss (46% of defeats), Conglomeration of Spheres (12% of defeats), O'Bannion's Thug (11% of defeats).
- **In campaign order** the second scenario sees the first: 1a played second (Dormitories, R2 path) wins 2p 37%, 3p 25%, 4p 28%; 1b played second (Peter Clover / Fold) wins 2p 5%, 3p 12%, 4p 17%.
- These are the AI's rates. The downloaded data holds no human win rates (see *Reference points*), so how far they sit below a group of people cannot be quoted. The AI is the same one The Still Hour's balance runs use; docs/design/BENCHMARK_DUNWICH_VS_STILL_HOUR.md reads The Still Hour against these numbers.

## Campaign order (both scenarios, the second sees the first)

`pair_ab` plays 1a then 1b, `pair_ba` 1b then 1a. Between them the engine applies the first scenario's resolution from the guide data: log entries, chaos tokens (+[tablet] if the students were not saved, +[elder_thing] if the party cheated), trauma (each point starts the next scenario as 1 damage or horror), an earned ally added to a deck (Rice to Rex, Morgan to Zoey), and the XP spent on the same upgrade path the Still Hour's same-class deck uses (tools/play_engine/decks.py UPGRADES). The second scenario then plays its "completed the other scenario" setup (1a: Faculty Offices *The Hour is Late* (no R1), the Alchemical Concoction under the Alchemy Labs, agenda 1 straight to 2b; 1b: Peter Clover and *Fold*, agenda 1 straight to 2b). Armitage's Fate (the interlude) is then read from the log: kidnapped if either scenario recorded *unconscious for several hours* (each investigator +2 XP), else rescued.

## Fidelity: what is approximated, what is missing, how the AI differs

Approximated or simplified (from the effects table's coverage notes):

- Locked Door: full; attached to the revealed location with the most clues (ties: the first found)
- Yithian Observer: full; the random discard happens after the attack is dealt (if the hand was empty it deals +1/+1)
- Offer of Power: full; the AI takes 2 horror unless that leaves it at 2 sanity or less, or the agenda is far from its threshold
- Dr. Henry Armitage: not played in either scenario (Armitage's Fate decides who may add him)
- Extracurricular Activity: full (Standard side); the [elder_thing] modifier is the discarded cards' printed cost (the AI estimates it before the reveal)
- Dead of Night: full; the hand size of 5 is checked at the end of the upkeep phase
- Rice's Whereabouts: full; the AI spends a clue on its [action] while "Jazz" Mulligan has not been found
- Miskatonic Quad: full; the AI never resigns in Extracurricular Activity (the no-resolution path is reached by defeat only)
- The Experiment: full; Massive is the engine's (it attacks each investigator at its location)
- Professor Warren Rice: full; added to a deck only in campaign-order runs
- The House Always Wins: full (Standard side); a [skull] is treated as 0 for 2 resources only when that turns a failure into a success
- Beginner's Luck: full; "cheating" is a policy switch (cfg.cheat), off by default
- La Bella Luna: full; the AI resigns when its act 3 objective is done or the last agenda is about to advance
- Clover Club Cardroom: full; the AI never "cheats" with Beginner's Luck unless the run asks it to
- Back Alley: full; see La Bella Luna
- Light of Aforgomon: full; attached to the act unless the act already has one (the AI's choice)
- Wizard of Yog-Sothoth: full; its attack on a drawn [[Hex]]/[[Pact]] card happens before that card resolves
- Whippoorwill: full; the AI attacks it only when it is at the investigator's location (it is aloof)
- O'Bannion's Thug: full; the resource it denies is the upkeep phase's (other gains are not blocked)
- Pushed into the Beyond: full; the asset shuffled back is the least valuable one
- Terror from Beyond: full; the chosen card types are those that cost the party the fewest cards
- Arcane Barrier: full; on a failed test the AI discards the top 5 cards and moves (deck of 10 or more, no Beyond the Veil) or cancels the move
- Jenny Barnes: full; the extra resource is added at the end of the upkeep phase
- Jenny's Twin .45s: X is all her resources but 1 (at most 5)
- Jim Culver: full; he never treats an [elder_sign] as a [skull] (his [skull] is 0 but his [elder_sign] is +1)
- Rex Murphy: full; the [elder_sign] is always taken as +2 (never the auto-fail for 3 cards); his reaction has no limit (as ArkhamDB's text prints it)
- Rex's Curse: on a success the token is re-revealed once; the first token's effects and the committed cards' success effects are not undone
- Search for the Truth: played with 2+ clues and room in hand
- Searching for Izzie: the AI goes for it when no objective is pressing

Engine-wide:

- The AI is The Still Hour's greedy one-action scorer. It plans no further than the action at hand and the next location; it often evades an enemy that will re-engage at the next upkeep instead of killing it, investigates at long odds rather than walking to a better location, and only knows a scenario's tricks where they were written into `dwl_ai.lua` (e.g. not to gain clues next to the Pit Boss, to keep "Jazz" out of harm, not to draw with Beyond the Veil on a thin deck). People talk, plan rounds ahead, mulligan for the scenario, hold cards for its threats and resign when a scenario turns: they win these more often.
- Decks are fixed level-0 lists built for The Still Hour (Guardian/Seeker/Rogue/Mystic starter cards), not decks a player would build for Dunwich; no Fortune cards for Rex (none in the list).
- Player cards are engine data (hand, deck, discard), not cards on the table; damage, horror, resources and clues on investigators are tracked by the engine (the clue tokens are moved to the playmats).
- Who goes where is decided per investigator each action; the party never splits a test between several turns on purpose. Choices printed on cards are made by fixed policies (listed above).
- In 1a the AI never resigns at the Quad (no-resolution games are total defeats). In 1b it resigns at La Bella Luna or the Back Alley once act 3's NPC is controlled or lost, or when the last agenda is one doom from its threshold (plus one per three steps it must walk).
- Standard difficulty only; one random basic weakness from The Still Hour engine's encoded list (core and Dunwich basic weaknesses).

## Reference points

The downloaded data (ArkhamDB card data, arkham-cards-data's campaign guide, SCED's box) holds no play statistics: no win rates or difficulty ratings for these scenarios, so none is quoted here. The data does fix the scenarios' own budgets, which the table above can be read against: 1a's agendas allow 10 doom before the last (looping) agenda, its acts ask 3 clues per investigator before the location objectives (2 at the Faculty Offices, 3 at the Dormitories); 1b's agendas allow 14 doom in all, its acts ask 6 clues per investigator.
