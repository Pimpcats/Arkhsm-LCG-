# THE STILL HOUR — Campaign Guide
*A campaign for 1–4 investigators, best with 3.*

---

## HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

This guide is read as you play. **Do not read ahead.** It tells you when to read each section: the Prologue first, then the loop, then the districts as you reach them. Story text in boxes is read aloud. Everything after a **"Do not read until…"** line stays unread until the guide sends you there.

The Still Hour is not a line of separate scenarios. It is **one night, lived over and over.** You will play the Prologue once, then play the night again and again (each play-through is a **loop**), learning a little more of it each time, until you know enough to end it. Every loop costs you years.

---

## CAMPAIGN RULES

**Loops.** A loop is one continuous play-through of the night across the shared Ambergrove map. The **Hourglass** (the Occultation clock, Hours I–IX) is the master timer for the whole loop. When the Hourglass reaches **Hour IX — The Appointed Hour**, when every investigator is defeated, or when Dissonance reaches **18**, the loop ends and the night **resets**. The board wipes and everyone is healed, but **Memory, the Knowledge Track, Dissonance's scar and Years all carry forward.**

**The board persists within a loop.** Damage, horror, resources and cards in play do **not** reset when you travel between districts. The reset is your only breather, and your only checkpoint.

**Districts and travel.** Ambergrove is a small inland town of six **districts**. Each is a cluster of locations with things to learn and do, set out from its own box. **The Square** is the hub. Travelling along a connection between districts costs **1 Hour** (a Skip). You will only reach 2 or 3 districts before time runs out.

**The Hourglass.** The clock advances about one Hour of its own accord each round (each Hour agenda has a doom threshold of 1), plus any **Skip** effects. When the Hourglass reaches an Hour, resolve that Hour's text.

**Dissonance.** A shared track from 0 to 18. It rises when you act on foreknowledge: playing Recollections, using foreknowledge abilities, and certain encounter cards. It only falls through specific cards. It sets the chaos bag's **band**:

| Band | Dissonance (3 investigators) | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Calm | 0–5 | Echoes are Sleepwalking. |
| Glitch | 6–11 | 1 [static] token in the chaos bag. Echoes wake. |
| Noticed | 12–17 | 2 [static] tokens in the chaos bag. The Appointed has Arrived. |
| Reset | 18 | The loop ends at once. |

A **[static]** token is −3; when it is revealed, raise Dissonance by 1. The Control token tracks Dissonance and manages the band's [static] tokens for you.

**Memory.** Memory tokens you earn during a loop sit on your cards. Between loops they are **banked**. Banked Memory is capped at **18** (6 × investigators) at the start of each loop. It is spent like experience: on **Recollections** at their listed Memory cost, and on card upgrades at 1 Memory per level. On-card Memory (fuel during a loop) and banked Memory (the campaign's currency) are always named separately.

**The Knowledge Track.** The night can be learned. Completing a district's objectives records **Knowledge** in your Campaign Log, and each piece of Knowledge permanently changes the night from then on. Knowledge is never bought; it is only found.

**Years.** At each reset every investigator gains **Years**: +1 always, +1 if they were defeated that loop, +1 if the loop ended at Dissonance 12 or higher, and +1 if they leaned on the loop (raised Dissonance 3 or more times, or spent 4 or more Memory). Years set an investigator's age bracket: **Prime 0–4, Weathered 5–9, Elder 10–14, Ancient 15+**, each with its own drift. An investigator who reaches **18 Years** ages out of the campaign.

**Echoes and the Appointed.** Echoes are **Sleepwalking** while Dissonance is Calm and wake in the Glitch band or higher. Something called **the Appointed** climbs an **Approach** (Unseen, Sensed, Emerging, Arrived), driven up by the clock and by Dissonance. It cannot be defeated, only **Held Back**: pushed back one stage, rewinding the Hourglass by an Hour.

**The Named (Victory).** From Act II, some districts add a **Named** Elite enemy; each district's entry says whether it is set aside until a card spawns it or shuffled into the encounter deck. Defeating one records its name on your Campaign Log and banks its **Victory X** as Memory, once per campaign per name. The Named return every loop like everything else in Ambergrove; only the first lesson pays.

**Acts of the campaign.**
- **Act I — Learning the Rules:** only each district's first objective (act 1a) is available.
- **Act II — The Shape of the Hour:** each district's second objective (act 2a) opens, the Appointed's Whispers join the encounter deck, and the Named enter the night. The guide tells you when Act II begins.
- **The Last Hour:** the finale, once your Campaign Log records **The Way the Night Breaks.**

## CAMPAIGN SETUP

1. **Choose investigators.** The campaign is tuned for 3; it scales from 1 to 4.
2. **Build decks.** Each investigator builds a deck under their deckbuilding rules.
3. **Choose a difficulty** and assemble the chaos bag:

| Difficulty | Chaos tokens |
|---|---|
| Easy | +1, +1, 0, 0, 0, −1, −1, −1, −2, −2, [skull], [skull], [cultist], [tablet], [elderthing], [autofail], [elder] |
| Standard | +1, 0, 0, −1, −1, −1, −2, −2, −3, −4, [skull], [skull], [cultist], [tablet], [elderthing], [autofail], [elder] |
| Hard | 0, 0, 0, −1, −1, −2, −2, −3, −3, −4, −5, [skull], [skull], [cultist], [tablet], [elderthing], [autofail], [elder] |
| Expert | 0, −1, −1, −2, −2, −3, −3, −4, −4, −5, −6, −8, [skull], [skull], [cultist], [tablet], [elderthing], [autofail], [elder] |

4. **Take the Campaign Log** (the log token, or the pages at the back of this guide). Record your investigators and difficulty. Each investigator starts at **0 Years** and **0 banked Memory**.
5. Turn to **Prologue — The First Hour.**

## THE AMBERGROVE MAP

A small inland town under a starless, snagged sky. **The Square** is the hub; travelling along a connection costs **1 Hour** (a Skip).

```
                 THE LIGHTHOUSE
                       |
                 THE SUNKEN ROAD
                       |
   THE FAIRGROUND — THE SQUARE — THE DROWNED CHURCH
                       |
                 THE ALMANAC HOUSE
```

- **The Square** connects to the Fairground, the Drowned Church, the Almanac House and the Sunken Road.
- **The Sunken Road** connects the Square to the **Lighthouse**.
- Each district is a cluster of 2–4 locations, revealed when you first enter it in a loop.

## PROLOGUE — THE FIRST HOUR

> You came to Ambergrove for your own reasons: a letter, a debt, a promise, a rumour. You will not remember them, soon.
>
> The whole town has turned out for the occultation. Lanterns are strung across the Square. A band is tuning up by the Town Hall steps, and children ride their fathers' shoulders, pointing at a sky that is not quite right.
>
> The almanac promised the occultation at midnight. It is eleven, and the stars are already sliding behind something that has no edge. You will remember everything else.

**Setup**
1. Press **Place** on **The First Hour** box. It lays out:
   - the **Hours** as the agenda deck, Hour I on top;
   - the act **The First Hour**;
   - **The Still Hour** scenario reference card;
   - the encounter deck, face down. Shuffle it;
   - one story card, set aside. Do not read it yet.
2. Put **The Square**, **The Long Pier** and **The Almanac Steps** into play in a row (Place does this). Each investigator begins at **The Square**.
3. On the Control token, set the Hourglass to **Hour I** and Dissonance to **0**. The chaos bag is in the Calm band.
4. Each investigator draws their opening hand. You are ready to begin.

> **Note.** The Prologue teaches the loop. It cannot be lost: however it ends, the night resets, and how well you did decides one small reward.

**Do not read until the end of the scenario.**

```resolution Resolution 1 — You Saw It Coming
> You were standing on the right step at the right second. You watched the occultation complete, and you watched something reach through, and not finish reaching. When the night folds, you fold with it on purpose, and you keep more of it.
- Each investigator gains **2 banked Memory**, and the party gains **1** more banked Memory.
- In your Campaign Log, record **You Are Unstuck.** Act I begins.
- No Years are gained from the Prologue.
- Turn to **The Loop**, read **Between Loops**, then begin Loop 1.
```

```resolution Resolution 2 — It Caught You Unready
> The occultation completes. Something reaches through, and does not finish reaching. The night folds. You are standing in the Square again at eleven o'clock, salt on your tongue, and you are the only ones who feel the seam.
- Each investigator gains **2 banked Memory.**
- In your Campaign Log, record **You Are Unstuck.** Act I begins.
- No Years are gained from the Prologue.
- Turn to **The Loop**, read **Between Loops**, then begin Loop 1.
```

## THE LOOP

Play every loop from here on the same way: set it up, play the night across the districts you choose, and when it ends read the loop's resolution, then **Between Loops**.

**Loop Setup**
1. If a loop just ended, press **Recall** on every box you placed. The board wipes; every investigator is healed of all damage and horror and returns their cards to their deck. Each investigator draws a new opening hand.
2. Press **Place** on **The Square (Town Hall)** box. It lays out the Square's locations, the **Hours** (agenda deck, Hour I on top), the scenario reference card, the shared encounter deck, the Square's act deck (in the district row between the mythos area and the map) and, set aside, the Appointed's cards.
3. Apply every Knowledge entry on your Campaign Log that changes the Hours (for example, an Hour removed from the Occultation is taken out now).
4. On the Control token, set the Hourglass to **Hour I** and Dissonance to the **scar**: the number of loops completed so far, maximum **6**.
5. **Choose the districts** you mean to visit this loop. For each one, press **Place** on its box: its locations join the map, its act deck takes its own spot in the district row, and its encounter set and any Named enemy go face down just below the mythos area. Shuffle each district's encounter set into the encounter deck. Leave each Named enemy set aside.
6. **Act II onward:** shuffle the Appointed's Whispers into the encounter deck.
7. The Appointed begins **Unseen**, set aside. Each investigator begins at **The Square**.
8. The first time you enter a district in this loop, read its entry in **The Districts**.

> Salt on your tongue. Lanterns across the Square. A band tuning up. It is eleven o'clock, and you have been here before.

**Do not read until the loop ends.**

A loop ends the moment one of its endings happens. Check them **top to bottom** and read the first that happened.

```resolution Resolution 2 — The Seam Tears (Dissonance reached 18)
> You leaned on what you remembered until the night could feel the weight. Every clock in Ambergrove stops on the same wrong second. The sky does not fold this time. It splits, and you fall back through the gap to eleven o'clock, bleeding time.
- The loop ended in danger: each investigator's **+1 Year** for Dissonance 12 or higher applies.
- In your Campaign Log, mark this loop **Torn**.
- If this is the **second or later** Torn loop, read this instead of the usual interlude story: *The town has learned your shape. Strangers at the fair stop mid-laugh when you pass, and do not know why.*
- Proceed to **Between Loops.**
```

```resolution No Resolution — The Night Keeps You (every investigator was defeated)
> The dark finishes what it started. For a long moment there is nothing: no Square, no bell, no salt. Then the lanterns are being lit again, and you are standing where you always stand, and your hands will not stop shaking.
- Each investigator gains the **+1 Year** for being defeated.
- In your Campaign Log, mark this loop **Taken**.
- If this is the **second or later** Taken loop, read this instead of the usual interlude story: *You have died in this town more times than you can hold in your head at once. Each of you keeps one of those deaths close, like a stone in a pocket, and it is heavier every time.*
- Proceed to **Between Loops.**
```

```resolution Resolution 1 — The Appointed Hour (the Hourglass reached Hour IX)
> The occultation completes on time, exactly as the almanac promised. Something reaches through the edgeless dark, and, again, does not finish reaching. The night folds along its old crease. Eleven o'clock. Salt. The lanterns.
- In your Campaign Log, mark this loop **Closed at the Hour**.
- If you recorded **at least one** new Knowledge entry this loop, add to the interlude story: *It is the first night that felt like progress. You hold on to that.* If you recorded **none**, add: *Another night spent. The town did not notice you at all, which is its own kind of warning.*
- Proceed to **Between Loops.**
```

## BETWEEN LOOPS

Run this after every reset, and once after the Prologue.

1. **Age.** Each investigator adds their Years for the loop just ended (see Campaign Rules). Apply any bracket change now; the first time an investigator enters Weathered, choose their locked drift on the Campaign Log. An investigator who reaches 18 Years ages out.
2. **Bank Memory.** Move all Memory on cards to banked Memory. Reduce banked Memory to **18** if it is higher.
3. **Spend.** Spend banked Memory from the shared pool: buy Recollections at their listed Memory cost, and level up eligible cards at 1 Memory per level, within each investigator's deckbuilding access. Upgraded cards replace their base versions.
4. **Record.** Update the scar (loops completed, maximum 6), the Knowledge Track, the dead and any choices on your Campaign Log.
5. **Check the Act.** If your log now holds **3 or more** Knowledge entries from districts' first objectives, or you have completed **Loop 3**, and Act II has not begun: Act II begins. Read **The Shape of the Hour**.
6. **Read** the interlude story below, then begin the next loop at **Loop Setup**.

> **After the Prologue and the first loop.** You wake at First Dark with the taste of salt and the certainty that you have done this before. The others feel it too. No one in Ambergrove will believe you.

> **The Shape of the Hour** (when Act II begins). The town has begun to notice that you notice. Somewhere past the edge of the Square, something that has been waiting politely decides to stop waiting. From the next loop, each district's second objective opens, the Appointed's Whispers join the encounter deck, and the Named enter the night.

> **Before the finale** (once your log records The Way the Night Breaks). You know the shape of the night now. You know what it costs to end it. The only question left is who pays. You may attempt **The Last Hour** during any loop from now on, or keep looping to prepare, knowing each loop costs years.

## THE DISTRICTS

Read a district's entry the first time you enter it in a loop. Its objectives are printed on its act deck: act 1a is always available, act 2a only in Act II. When an act tells you to record something in your Campaign Log, do so at once. It lasts through every reset.

### The Lighthouse

> The lamp at the end of the Sunken Road is dark. It has never, in any loop, been lit, and the keeper cannot remember why he didn't light it. The stair winds up into the smell of old oil and older salt.

**When you Place this box:** The Lantern Room, The Winding Stair and The Keeper's Quarters join the map. Shuffle its encounter set (The Dark That Waits, Something on the Stair) into the encounter deck. Its act deck: **Light the Lamp** (1a) and **The Ninth Death** (2a).

### The Drowned Church

> The bell tolled thirteen times at the first occultation. It should toll twelve. Water stands in the nave to the height of the pews, and the congregation is still kneeling in it.

**When you Place this box:** The Nave, The Belfry, The Vestry and The Flooded Crypt join the map. The Flooded Crypt stays sealed until a card opens it. Shuffle its encounter set (The Drowned Choir, Thirteen, Rising Water) into the encounter deck. Its act deck: **Why Thirteen?** (1a) and **The Hour Was Wrong** (2a). **Act II:** its Named enemy stays set aside until its act spawns it.

### The Sunken Road

> The road between the town and the shore. The same figures walk it every loop, in the same direction, hoods up, never arriving. Some of them turn to look at you now.

**When you Place this box:** The Milestones, The Low Bridge and The Turning join the map. Shuffle its encounter set (The Waiting Congregation, The Bridge Remembers) into the encounter deck. Its act deck: **Walk It Backward** (1a) and **Who Walks Beside You** (2a).

### The Square

> The hub of the night. The sheriff stands on the Town Hall steps giving the same speech to the same crowd. He has been dead since ten o'clock. No one has noticed but you.

**Every loop:** the Square's box is placed in Loop Setup, so its locations, encounter set (The Same Speech, The Crowd Turns) and act deck are always in play. Its act deck: **The Sheriff Is Already Dead** (1a) and **The Vote That Never Ends** (2a). **Act II:** its Named enemy stays set aside until its act spawns it.

### The Fairground

> The occultation fair, mid-celebration, forever. The wheel turns. The music never finishes its tune. The ticket-taker smiles at you like he has been expecting you for a very long time.

**When you Place this box:** The Wheel, The Hall of Mirrors and The Ticket Booth join the map. Shuffle its encounter set (The Wheel's Turn, Your Reflection Lies) into the encounter deck. Its act deck: **The Wheel Still Turns** (1a) and **The Ticket-Taker's Bargain** (2a). **Act II:** shuffle its Named enemy into the encounter deck with its encounter set; it spawns at **The Wheel** when drawn.

### The Almanac House

> Where the wrong almanac was printed. Stacks of it still wait by the press, bound and dated, the hour of the occultation set in fresh black type. At the back, a study door that no key in Ambergrove fits.

**When you Place this box:** The Reading Room, The Press and The Sealed Study join the map. The Sealed Study stays sealed until a card opens it. Shuffle its encounter set (The Page That Wasn't, Ink Runs Backward, The Study Door) into the encounter deck. Its act deck: **What the Almanac Hid** (1a) and **The Appointed's Name** (2a).

## FINALE — THE LAST HOUR

**Only once your Campaign Log records The Way the Night Breaks.** During any loop from then on, an investigator at **The Sealed Study** may declare **The Last Hour**. If the Hourglass reaches Hour IX while your log records The Way the Night Breaks, you may begin the finale then instead of resetting.

> You have lived this night more times than you can hold. You know the toll, the road, the wrong sky, the name. You know what the town agreed to: an appointment made in ink and never kept. All that is left is to keep it, and to decide what you are willing to spend.

**Setup**
1. Press **Place** on **The Last Hour** box. Its act, **Contest the Crossing**, takes the act spot; its set-aside stack goes to the mythos area's lower corner. Do not read the set-aside cards.
2. Resolve **Contest the Crossing**'s opening text: the Appointed's Approach advances to Arrived, and it manifests at the location farthest from the investigators if it is not already on the board. **Dissonance does not reset.** If the finale began at Hour IX, set the Hourglass back to Hour V; no Hour resolves.
3. The objective is to **contest the crossing**: gain contest progress equal to **4 × investigators (12 at three players)** before the Hourglass reaches Hour IX. Each **Hold Back** success, each deep Knowledge entry spent and each investigator's first arrival at The Sealed Study during the finale adds 1.

**Do not read until the end of the finale.**

When the finale ends, check the resolutions **top to bottom** and read the first that qualifies. If the contest was reached, begin with Resolution 1; if not, begin with Resolution 5.

```resolution Resolution 1 — Take Its Place (contest reached, and an Ancient investigator is present)
> Someone has to hold the night's crease closed from the inside. The oldest of you knows it before anyone speaks.
- One Ancient investigator becomes the night's new anchor: kept behind, decades older, so the loop ends for everyone else. Mark them **kept as anchor** on your Campaign Log.
- If your log records **Who Walks Beside You**, instead: the ones who walk the Sunken Road take the anchor's weight with you. At dawn the Ancient walks out with the others, **+3 Years**, but free and not kept.
- The town wakes for the first time in a hundred years. The campaign is over.
```

```resolution Resolution 1b — Let It In, On Your Terms (contest reached, your log records The Ticket-Taker's Bargain, and banked Memory is 12 or more; you may decline this and read on)
> He punches your ticket without looking. He has been expecting you for a very long time.
- The door opens on your terms, not the town's, and the night finally ends.
- The fare is everything you carried: banked Memory drops to **0**, and each investigator present ages **+2 Years**.
- Ambergrove wakes to a morning with one more guest in it. The campaign is over.
```

```resolution Resolution 2 — Close the Door (contest reached, and your log records The Vote That Never Ends and The Appointed's Name)
> You pay the price the town agreed to and never paid.
- Everyone present ages **+3 Years** at once, and the door closes.
- The cleanest true ending, bought with years. The campaign is over.
```

```resolution Resolution 3 — Break Through (contest reached, and your log records The Keeper's Ninth Death)
> You force the night open, and every survivor runs through before it can close.
- All surviving investigators escape. The campaign is over.
- But the loop is only paused. Was it really ended?
```

```resolution Resolution 4 — Seal by Force (contest reached)
> The door is sealed, but not closed.
- Choose one investigator. They age out holding it shut. The campaign is over.
```

```resolution Resolution 5 — Next Time (contest not reached, banked Memory 12 or more)
> The loop wins tonight, but you wake at First Dark closer than ever.
- Proceed to **Between Loops**, then begin a new loop. You may attempt the finale again.
```

```resolution Resolution 6 — The Loop Wins (contest not reached, banked Memory below 12)
> The night takes you. The town keeps repeating, and somewhere on the Sunken Road, walking the same direction and never arriving, there are new faces beneath the hoods.
- The campaign ends.
```

## DIFFICULTY AND PLAYER COUNT

- **3 investigators:** every value as printed (Dissonance bands at 6 / 12, reset at 18, Memory cap 18, contest 12, scar cap 6).
- **2 or 4 investigators:** the reset comes at **6 × investigators**; the Glitch band starts at one-third of the reset and the Noticed band at two-thirds (**4 × investigators**). The Memory cap is **6 × investigators**, the contest is **4 × investigators**, and the scar cap is one-third of the reset.
- **1 investigator:** the reset comes at **9** and the Noticed band at **6**; Glitch starts at **3**. The Memory cap is **6**, the contest **4**, the scar cap **3**.
- The Control token applies all of these for you.
- The campaign is tuned for about 6–8 loops.
