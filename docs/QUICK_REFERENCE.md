# THE STILL HOUR — Quick Reference

> **Play aid. Read it when you sit down to play.** It explains how the
> campaign's systems work (the same ground as the guide's **New Rules**), so it
> is not for reading ahead. It has no story: no plot, no endings, no enemy
> beyond the names on the Control token. When this page and the campaign guide
> differ, **the guide governs**. Numbers are for **three investigators**; the
> Control token applies the values for other counts (guide: **Difficulty and
> Player Count**).

## A round

| Phase | As usual, plus |
|---|---|
| **Mythos** (from round 2) | Place 1 doom on the current **Hour**, then check its threshold. If the Hour passes: remove all doom in play, turn the Hours deck and click **Hour** on the Control token. *Guide: Campaign Rules, The Hourglass.* |
| **Investigation** | The first move each round along a connection between two districts places 1 doom on the current Hour (**crossing doom**). *Guide: Districts and travel.* |
| **Enemy** | If the Appointed hunts and is ready and unengaged, click **Hunt** once. *Guide: The Control token, The Appointed.* |
| **Upkeep** | As usual. |

Whenever an effect changes Dissonance, Memory on cards or Years, click the
matching Control or investigator button (*guide: The Control token*).

## The campaign's terms

- **Loop.** One play of the night. It ends when the Hourglass reaches Hour IX,
  when every investigator is defeated or has resigned, or when Dissonance
  reaches 24. Then read the loop's resolution and **Between Loops**. *Guide:
  The Loop; Between Loops.*
- **Hourglass / Hours.** The agenda deck: nine Hours, I to IX. Hour I needs 3
  doom, Hours II–VIII need 2. Each time an Hour passes, each investigator heals
  1 damage and 1 horror. Hours II–IX each have a "Forced – When the Hourglass advances to
  this Hour" ability that resolves as it arrives. A **Skip** advances Hours at once,
  resolving each in order; a **rewind** goes back without resolving any Forced
  ability. *Guide: Campaign Rules, The Hourglass.*
- **Crossing doom.** Each round, the first move (by anyone, either way, by
  any means) along each connection between two districts places 1 doom on the
  current Hour; later moves along it that round are free. Enemy moves never
  cost time. One connection on the map never costs time. *Guide: Districts and
  travel; The Ambergrove Map.*
- **Dissonance and its bands.** One shared number, 0–24; it changes only when
  an effect raises or lowers it. **Calm** 0–7: Echoes are Sleepwalking.
  **Glitch** 8–15: 1 Static token in the bag; entering it moves the Approach to at least
  Sensed. **Noticed** 16–23: 2 Static tokens; entering it moves the Approach to Arrived;
  each investigator ages 1 extra Year at the reset. **24**: the loop ends at
  once. Each loop starts at the **scar** (loops completed, up to 6). *Guide:
  Campaign Rules, Dissonance.*
- **Static ([static]).** A chaos token: **−3, and raise Dissonance by 1** when
  revealed in a skill test. Its band decides how many are in the bag. When one
  is drawn, finish any cancel choices, then click **Resolve** on the token
  before you continue (do not also click Dissonance). *Guide: [static]; The
  Control token, Dissonance.*
- **Sleepwalking.** While Sleepwalking, an enemy cannot engage, attack, move or
  be attacked, evaded or targeted. Echoes are Sleepwalking only in the Calm
  band; an Echo that wakes is exhausted. *Guide: Echoes and Sleepwalking.*
- **The Approach.** The Appointed climbs Unseen, Sensed, Emerging, Arrived;
  bands and some Hours push it up, and lowering Dissonance never pushes it
  back. It cannot be defeated. *Guide: Campaign Rules, The Appointed.*
- **Hold Back.** An [action] on the Appointed's card, for an investigator at
  its location while it is ready. A success exhausts it, pushes the Approach
  back 1 stage and rewinds the Hourglass 1 Hour (at most 3 rewinds a loop;
  click **Hold Back** on its card, which also rewinds the Hour counter). *Guide:
  The Appointed; The Control token, The Appointed.*
- **Memory.** Your experience. Memory on your cards is banked into one shared
  pool at the reset and spent Between Loops on Recollections (their printed
  cost, no extra 1) and upgrades (the new card's
  level, with no credit for the card replaced: level 0 to 2 to 4 costs 6, level
  0 straight to 4 costs 4; any other new level 0 card costs 1). After spending,
  banked Memory above 10 per investigator (30 at three) is cut to that number.
  Whoever carries the most Memory on their cards is the one hunted (ties: the
  nearest, then the lead investigator chooses). *Guide: Campaign Rules, Memory.*
- **Knowledge.** Completing a district's act records a Knowledge entry; it is
  found, never bought, and changes the town from then on. The first record
  banks 1 Memory per investigator (surface entry) or 3 (deep entry). Tick it on
  the Campaign Log token. *Guide: Campaign Rules, Knowledge.*
- **Years.** No trauma, no deaths. At each reset each investigator gains 1
  Year, +1 if defeated, +1 if the loop ended in Noticed or higher, +1 if they
  **leaned on the loop** (paid "raise Dissonance" as a cost 3+ times, or spent
  4+ of their own Memory on their own cards). Brackets: Prime 0–4, Weathered
  5–9, Elder 10–14, Ancient 15+. At 18 an investigator ages out. *Guide:
  Campaign Rules, Years.*
- **Resign.** "[action]: Resign" is on the hub Square only. You are
  eliminated for the rest of the loop, but it is not a defeat (no Year for
  it), and Memory on your cards is still banked. *Guide: Resigning.*
- **Victory X.** Pays once per campaign: when you defeat an enemy with Victory
  X, or a loop ends with a Victory X location in play, revealed and with no
  clues on it, record it on the Victory list and each investigator gains X
  banked Memory. Claiming one costs time. *Guide: Campaign Rules, Victory.*
- **Parts.** Part II begins once the log records 3 surface Knowledge entries or
  after Loop 3; each district's second objective then opens. *Guide: Parts of
  the campaign; Between Loops, step 5.*

## Between loops, in order

Click **Reset Loop**, then **Interlude**: 1 Age, 2 Bank Memory, 3 Spend,
4 Record, 5 Check the Part, 6 Read, then **Begin Next Loop** and **Clear
Board**, and set up at **Loop Setup**. *Guide: Between Loops; The Loop.*
