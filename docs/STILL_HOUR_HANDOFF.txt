DIFFICULTY (model results, not human win probabilities; Standard, 3 investigators)
The first calibration tested each night from a fresh setup (table below). Played as whole campaigns, those nights had one goal after the Square was done and won 90-100%, so the whole-campaign runs now give every night two goals, as official scenarios have about three acts, and the guide tells players to plan on about two acts a night. Act costs were then set from those runs (docs/design/FINISHING_BALANCE.md, "Whole-campaign calibration"). Whole-campaign result, 20 campaigns: mean night win 59% against the 60% target mean; individual nights swing widely (20% to 90%) because each depends on which districts it draws. Finale on the final cards: 32% (13 of 40) against 40%. Experience per investigator over a campaign: 36 to 49 across four parties.

HOW TO LOAD
1. Download dist/saved_object_the_still_hour.json from main.
2. Copy it into Documents\My Games\Tabletop Simulator\Saves\Saved Objects\.
3. Load SCED, then Objects > Saved Objects > The Still Hour, and press Place.
4. Open the included guide and follow Campaign Setup.
Detailed loading instructions: docs/LOADING.md.
To move a campaign in progress onto a newer build without losing progress, follow "Updating to a newer build" in docs/LOADING.md (keep the campaign log, swap the Control token and scenario boxes). Save your game in TTS at the end of every session.
