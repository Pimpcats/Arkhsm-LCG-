HOW TO LOAD
1. Download dist/saved_object_the_still_hour.json from main.
2. Copy it into Documents\My Games\Tabletop Simulator\Saves\Saved Objects\.
3. Load SCED, then Objects > Saved Objects > The Still Hour, and press Place.
4. Open the included guide and follow Campaign Setup.
Detailed loading instructions: docs/LOADING.md.
Before updating an existing campaign, save the table and preserve its campaign state; use the update instructions in that document.
